Weekly Retro #32

September 7, 2026

The week was about coming back, getting back to routine, and the comedown from the NYC trip. I was also getting over a cold that my wife eventually got.

It was an emotional week for me too. I'm not sure how much I want to get into the specifics, but I'll talk a little about what I've realized as a result. The short version is I spend a lot of time, too much time, in the future. I get into this mode where I'm predicting what will or won't happen, and things usually go negative fairly quickly. Sometimes, even after predicting a positive outcome, I'll return to it later and go the negative route instead. It makes any moment of joy ephemeral—I almost immediately start thinking about how I can get that feeling again, and mostly assume I won't be able to.

I tend to rehearse events ahead of time by going through the permutations of what I will and won't say and trying to predict the responses to those things. Trying to glean information about something that hasn't happened, and in some cases won't happen, when there's no real way to do that. So my brain invents. I run these experiments, though they aren't really experiments because there is no actual data. It's all imaginary.

This goes in reverse too. I revisit the past. I pick apart conversations and interactions trying to glean meaning or interpret intent when the present usually has the answers to those things.

About the name

This is a place where I'll post writing of my own. Mostly essays,short notes and my weekly retros. It's named after the White Stripes song of the same name. I heard it years ago, and I've used the name for various incarnations of my blog ever since. To me it perfectly captures the essence of what I'm doing here: good work, in my little room, that'll maybe turn into something bigger.
